This artwork uses a Perlin noise flow field to guide hundreds of particles along smooth, organic trajectories. Each particle traces a luminous curve through the invisible vector field, creating dense, river-like patterns.

The background layer uses low-frequency noise to create a subtle topographic texture, while the foreground curves layer with varying opacity to produce depth and luminosity. Inspired by Tyler Hobbs' flow field techniques and the Art Blocks generative art tradition.
