Lissajous curves are the family of parametric curves defined by x = A sin(at + d) and y = B sin(bt), where a and b are frequencies and d is the phase difference. When a/b is rational, the curve closes; when irrational, it fills a region densely. This artwork layers multiple curves with seed-varied frequency combinations, phase relationships, and amplitude modulations. A background dot grid provides spatial reference, while the overlapping curves create moire-like interference patterns with varying opacity and line weight.
