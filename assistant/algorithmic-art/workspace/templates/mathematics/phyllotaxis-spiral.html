Phyllotaxis describes the arrangement of leaves, seeds, or florets in plants. Each element is placed at a fixed divergence angle (approximately 137.508 degrees, the golden angle) from the previous one, at a distance proportional to the square root of its index. This produces Fibonacci spirals visible in sunflower heads. The angle offset parameter lets you explore how tiny deviations from the golden angle create dramatically different patterns. The seed controls palette shift and element shape variation, ensuring each generation is unique.
