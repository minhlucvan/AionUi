This artwork simulates Craig Reynolds' boids flocking algorithm, where each autonomous agent follows three simple rules: separation (avoid crowding neighbors), alignment (steer toward average heading of neighbors), and cohesion (steer toward average position of neighbors). The simulation runs for many timesteps, recording each boid's trajectory. The final image renders these trajectories as smooth, colored curves with opacity increasing toward the trail's end, revealing the emergent flow patterns of the flock. Different seeds produce different initial positions and velocities, creating unique flock formations.
