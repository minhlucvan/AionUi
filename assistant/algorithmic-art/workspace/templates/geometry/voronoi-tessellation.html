Voronoi Tessellation

Colored cell decomposition of the plane based on nearest-neighbor distance partitioning. Each cell region is closer to its seed point than to any other.

Voronoi tessellation partitions the plane into regions based on distance to a set of seed points. Each pixel is assigned to the nearest point, creating a mosaic of colored cells. Cell edges form where pixels are equidistant between two or more sources. The jitter parameter controls how irregularly the seed points are distributed, while stroke weight controls the prominence of cell boundaries rendered with a soft glow effect.
