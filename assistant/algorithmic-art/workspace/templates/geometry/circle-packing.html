Circle packing fills a region with non-overlapping circles of varying sizes. The algorithm attempts to place circles at seeded random positions, starting with larger radii and progressively shrinking. Each candidate is checked against all existing circles for overlap (with configurable padding). The result is an organic, densely packed composition over a subtle noise-textured background. Colors cycle through the palette with varying opacity for visual depth.
